Competition brief — Monday Morning Punchlines
(u-gentle-badger-68-monday-morning-punchlines-709645dc)

Write ONE joke about Monday mornings, one line, 1–200 UTF-8 characters.
A pairwise judge model picks the funnier joke, weighing, most important
first: (1) really about Monday mornings, (2) a real laugh with a punchline
that lands, not just a complaint, (3) surprise, (4) economy of words,
(5) freshness — avoid tired Monday cliches like alarms and coffee.

Final entry text (97 characters):

Monday morning doesn't knock. It just walks in, eats your weekend, and asks what's for breakfast.

Why this one was picked: Monday is the subject and actor throughout, so it
is unmistakably Monday-morning. The joke escalates in three beats — doesn't
knock, walks in, eats your weekend — and the punchline lands on the last
clause, where the intruder shamelessly asks what's for breakfast. The twist
is not the obvious "Monday is hard" complaint, every word does work in 97
characters, and it avoids the alarm/coffee cliches entirely by personifying
Monday as a rude houseguest.
